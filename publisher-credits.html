Publisher content and credits

NewsOrbit · Updated 24 October 2026

NewsOrbit identifies the publisher of each report, links to the original article and displays supplied author credits when available. Default summaries select publisher sentences locally. Optional on-device AI summaries and translations are labelled separately, can contain errors and are not publisher-authored or independently verified reporting. Generated wording is never presented as a publisher quotation.

Ownership and permission

Publisher headlines, articles, excerpts, photographs and trademarks belong to their respective rights holders. Source names identify the origin of content. Inclusion in a source directory does not imply affiliation, endorsement or a licence for any particular use.

Attribution, a public webpage and a working RSS feed do not by themselves grant permission to republish, adapt, narrate, cache or commercially reuse content. Publisher-specific terms, applicable permissions and law govern each use. This page does not grant rights to publisher material. Required permissions for public app distribution remain subject to review.

Optional discovery and local video

Google News public RSS discovery is optional. The originating publisher is credited and source rules still apply. Discovery headlines do not grant access or reuse rights to the full article. Multi-publisher coverage snippets are not treated as a verified, attributed article body.

Video summaries accept transcripts or local files the reader supplies. The app does not automatically download online videos or captions. The reader must have permission to process the selected material. Local AI and speech recognition can make mistakes; review the original recording.
