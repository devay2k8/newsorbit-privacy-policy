NewsOrbit — Privacy Policy

Updated 24 October 2026 · 0.2.0 alpha

Overview

NewsOrbit is a local-first Android news reader from PD Nexus. This policy describes the 0.2.0 alpha implementation. No registration, advertising, paid AI service or NewsOrbit cloud backend is required. SDK usage-metrics uploads are disabled in this build.

What stays on your device

NewsOrbit stores selected topics, source choices, manual region interests, custom feeds, article metadata and publisher-supplied excerpts in app-private storage. Bookmarks, optional reading/search history, source-check status and notification records also stay on this device. Public article text used for richer Story Detail summaries is kept in a disposable app-private cache: up to 32 entries, 48,000 characters per article, usable for 24 hours. Prepared speech and model runtime files use local cache. Optional downloaded models use app-private storage. Search vectors, generated summaries, translations, imported transcripts and recognized video speech are processed locally and held in memory, without a NewsOrbit cloud upload. Video-screen text and results are discarded when that screen closes. These records are not sent to PD Nexus.

Connections to news sources

When you refresh, the app connects directly over HTTPS to allowed RSS/Atom feed servers. Opening a story can also request its publicly accessible publisher page for a local summary. Enabling Google News discovery allows requests to its public RSS service, with the originating publisher credited; publisher rules still apply. Source operators and their hosting providers can see ordinary connection data such as your IP address, request time, requested URL and the app's user-agent. If images are enabled, image hosts receive image requests too. Wi-Fi-only applies to article retrieval; data saver prevents new article-page requests. Cached article text remains usable offline, and unavailable pages fall back to feed excerpts. Tapping Download in On-device models requests pinned model files from Google Storage or Hugging Face and their delivery providers, which may include Amazon Web Services. Those hosts receive ordinary connection information, not your inference input. Model downloads obey data saver and Wi-Fi-only settings and are verified against their expected size and SHA-256. Publisher and model-host terms and privacy policies apply independently. Article retrieval does not sign in, execute page scripts, or bypass paywalls or access controls.

Source choices and attribution

Source blocks and selected-only rules apply to the reader, search, summaries, briefings and alerts. Some cached saved items may remain stored while hidden by your current rules; clearing all local data removes them. Stories identify their publishers and link to the original reports. Publisher-supplied author credits are shown when available. Publisher headlines, excerpts, article text, images and trademarks remain the property of their respective rights holders. Credit, a public webpage or an RSS URL does not by itself grant a licence to republish, adapt or commercially reuse content, and does not imply publisher endorsement or affiliation. Publisher-specific terms, any required permissions and applicable law govern content use. Automated grouping and extractive sentence selection do not certify factual accuracy or independent verification.

Audio and AI

Default briefs select publisher sentences locally. Optional on-device AI summaries and English/Hindi translation use a separately downloaded Qwen language model. English meaning search uses a downloaded sentence encoder; keyword search remains available for Hindi. Video summaries accept transcripts you paste or import, or local video/audio files you explicitly select. A downloaded Whisper speech model transcribes selected recordings on the device; no online video or caption scraping is included. Use only material you have permission to process. Recognition, generated summaries, translations and similarity rankings can be inaccurate; review the transcript and credited original source. They are not publisher-authored or independently verified reporting. These features work offline after their models are installed; no input text, video or audio is sent to a cloud AI service. Optional listening uses an installed Android text-to-speech voice marked as offline; network-required voices are rejected. Voice installation and the installed speech engine are controlled by Android or its provider and may have separate privacy practices. Audio pauses in the background. No microphone recording is used. X data collection remains unavailable.

Permissions

Internet and network-state access support feed retrieval and optional model downloads. On Android versions requiring it, notification permission is requested only when you enable notifications. The app does not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or unrestricted storage access. You may enter optional region interests manually. Document import/export and video/audio selection use Android's file picker, which grants access to the chosen file. The app does not scan your media library. Automatic refresh is optional and may be delayed by Android.

Feedback, links and exports

Support uses your selected email application. NewsOrbit does not send a message itself or claim delivery. Optional app/device information is included only when you select it; article history and custom feeds are not attached automatically. Original articles, privacy links and voice settings open in external apps whose policies apply. You control where exported JSON preferences and WAV audio are saved; a chosen cloud document provider may upload those files under its own policy.

Retention and deletion

Unsaved news is pruned during refresh after approximately 14 days and bounded to a recent cache. Saved feed excerpts are retained until you remove them or delete all local data. The separate article-text cache expires after 24 hours and is limited to 32 entries; it is disposable and not guaranteed to retain saved full articles. Reading and search history can be disabled and cleared. Clear cache removes article text, cached images, unsaved news, search vectors and prepared audio while retaining bookmarks and downloaded models. You can remove individual models in On-device models. Delete all local data also removes downloaded models and resets settings. Uninstalling removes app-private data. Selected original media files are never deleted by NewsOrbit. Files exported elsewhere and emails you sent must be deleted separately. Android app backup is disabled in the manifest; device manufacturers' transfer behavior may vary.

Security and limitations

The app uses HTTPS, bounded XML parsing, rejects external entities, and limits network destinations. These safeguards reduce risk but are not a guarantee of perfect security. This is an alpha; emulator tests do not replace physical-device, security, publisher-rights or Play Store release validation. Never rely on a brief or model output as emergency, medical, financial or legal advice. Open the original report and seek appropriate professional or official guidance for high-stakes decisions.

Children and changes

NewsOrbit is a general-news reader, not designed specifically for children; external news can contain mature subject matter. We do not knowingly request children's personal information. This policy will be updated when the implementation changes. Future features that send data to a service must be disclosed before they are enabled.
