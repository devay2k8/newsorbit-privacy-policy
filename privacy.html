NewsOrbit — Privacy Policy

Updated 23 October 2026 · 0.1.0 alpha

Overview

NewsOrbit is a local-first Android news reader from PD Nexus. This policy describes the 0.1.0 alpha implementation, not features planned for future versions. No registration, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, paid AI service or NewsOrbit cloud backend is included.

What stays on your device

NewsOrbit stores selected topics, source choices, manual region interests, custom feeds, article metadata and publisher-supplied excerpts in app-private storage. Bookmarks, optional reading/search history, source-check status and notification records also stay on this device. Public article text used for richer Story Detail summaries is kept in a disposable app-private cache: up to 32 entries, 48,000 characters per article, usable for 24 hours. Prepared speech is also stored in local cache. These records are not sent to PD Nexus.

Connections to news sources

When you refresh, the app connects directly over HTTPS to selected RSS/Atom feed servers. Opening a story can also request its publicly accessible publisher page for an on-device extractive summary. Source operators and their hosting providers can see ordinary connection data such as your IP address, request time, requested URL and the app's user-agent. If article images are enabled, image hosts receive image requests too. Wi-Fi-only applies to article retrieval; data saver prevents new article-page requests. Cached article text remains usable offline, and unavailable pages fall back to feed excerpts. Publisher terms and privacy policies apply independently. Article retrieval does not sign in, execute page scripts, or bypass paywalls or access controls.

Source choices and attribution

Source blocks and selected-only rules apply to the reader, search, summaries, briefings and alerts. Some cached saved items may remain stored while hidden by your current rules; clearing all local data removes them. Stories link to their original publishers. Automated grouping is approximate and does not certify factual accuracy or independent verification.

Audio and AI

Briefs use local extractive sentence selection, not a cloud language model. Optional audio uses an installed Android text-to-speech voice marked as offline; voices requiring a network connection are rejected. Voice installation and the installed speech engine are controlled by Android or its provider and may have separate privacy practices. Audio pauses in the background. No speech microphone recording is used. Large generative models, X data collection, Google News integration, video summaries and translation are unavailable in this build.

Permissions

Internet and network-state access support feed retrieval. On Android versions requiring it, notification permission is requested only when you enable notifications. The app does not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or unrestricted storage access. You may enter optional region interests manually. Document import/export uses Android's file picker. Automatic refresh is optional and may be delayed by Android.

Feedback, links and exports

Support uses your selected email application. NewsOrbit does not send a message itself or claim delivery. Optional app/device information is included only when you select it; article history and custom feeds are not attached automatically. Original articles, privacy links and voice settings open in external apps whose policies apply. You control where exported JSON preferences and WAV audio are saved; a chosen cloud document provider may upload those files under its own policy.

Retention and deletion

Unsaved news is pruned during refresh after approximately 14 days and bounded to a recent cache. Saved feed excerpts are retained until you remove them or delete all local data. The separate article-text cache expires after 24 hours and is limited to 32 entries; it is disposable and not guaranteed to retain saved full articles. Reading and search history can be disabled and cleared. Clear cache removes article text, cached images, unsaved news and audio while retaining bookmarks; Delete all local data also clears article text. Uninstalling removes app-private data. Files exported elsewhere and emails you sent must be deleted separately. Android app backup is disabled in the manifest; device manufacturers' transfer behavior may vary.

Security and limitations

The app uses HTTPS, bounded XML parsing, rejects external entities, and limits network destinations. These safeguards reduce risk but are not a guarantee of perfect security. NewsOrbit has not yet completed Android device, security or Play Store release validation. Never rely on a brief as emergency, medical, financial or legal advice. Open the original report and seek appropriate professional or official guidance for high-stakes decisions.

Children and changes

NewsOrbit is a general-news reader, not designed specifically for children; external news can contain mature subject matter. We do not knowingly request children's personal information. This policy will be updated when the implementation changes. Future features that send data to a service must be disclosed before they are enabled.
